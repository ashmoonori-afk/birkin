# Outreach Script

Build cold email or sales outreach sequences that open with genuine value or insight, then gently introduce the offer. Prioritize relevance and permission over invasiveness.

## When to Use

- Cold email campaigns to prospects or partnerships.
- Outreach sequences (initial, follow-up, final).
- LinkedIn or direct-message scripts for B2B/B2C outreach.

## When NOT to Use

- Warm introductions or referral-based outreach (different tone).
- Transactional or automated email (use a transactional template instead).

## Always establish first

`target customer`, `problem they face`, `offer/value`, `channel` (email, LinkedIn, etc.), `proof or credibility hook`, and `CTA`. If any missing, ask or state assumptions.

## Procedure

1. **Open with insight or value**, not a pitch (e.g., research you found relevant to them, a specific problem in their industry).
2. **Connect to their world**: mention a detail that shows you know their context (role, company, recent news).
3. **Introduce the offer gently**: frame as a resource, conversation, or low-friction next step.
4. **One clear CTA**: "reply with a question," "book a 15-min call," "try the free tool."
5. **Create a sequence**: initial (cold), follow-up #1 (value re-frame), follow-up #2 (final soft ask).
6. **Keep tone conversational**: one person writing to another, not a template.

## Output

- Initial email (subject line + body) + 1–2 follow-ups.
- Specific personalization hooks to fill in.
- Assumptions about audience and proof.
- A/B suggestions (e.g., insight hook vs. problem hook).
